Your newsletters, read to you.

Paperboy turns the newsletters in your Gmail into one short audio brief, ready every morning.

How it works

  1. Connect Gmail. Sign in with Google and give Paperboy read-only access. It can never send, delete or change your email.
  2. Pick your newsletters. Paperboy finds the newsletters in your inbox and you choose which go into your brief. Nothing else is read.
  3. Listen each morning. At the time you choose, Paperboy summarises the latest issues into one episode in the voice and length you like, and lets you know it's ready.

Your inbox stays yours. Paperboy reads only the newsletters you pick, never uses your email for advertising, and lets you delete your account and everything with it from inside the app.
